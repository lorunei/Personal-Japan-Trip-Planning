# LORUNEI order workflow — v57

## Quick Help — $49

1. Customer chooses the $49 level and reviews the matching sample.
2. Customer pays through the live Stripe Payment Link. If payment is not enabled, the button opens the inquiry form.
3. Stripe redirects to `thank-you.html?plan=quick`.
4. Customer submits one focused topic, up to 3 closely related questions and relevant context through `intake.html?plan=quick`.
5. LORUNEI reconciles the Stripe email / reference with the intake submission.
6. Before deeper individualized work begins, LORUNEI checks that the request fits Quick Help.
7. If Japan Answer clearly fits better, customer may keep the $49 scope or approve applying the full $49 toward Japan Answer and pay the remaining $50.
8. LORUNEI sends the work-start confirmation.
9. Quick Help is usually delivered by email within 2 business days after the information required to begin has been received.

## Japan Answer — $99

1. Customer submits an inquiry describing one connected decision.
2. LORUNEI confirms scope and delivery timing in writing.
3. Customer pays through the link sent for the confirmed scope.
4. Customer completes the plan-specific intake.
5. LORUNEI sends the work-start confirmation, then delivers the Decision Brief as agreed.

## Japan Research — $299

Use the same inquiry-first flow. Confirm the exact topic, comparison criteria, number/type of named options, delivery format and delivery date before payment.

## Private Japan Planning — from $999

Use an individually written scope and quote. Confirm trip length, cities, deliverables, revision/support limits, timing and price before payment.

## Service boundary

LORUNEI provides research, comparison, information and planning. Customers make, contract and pay for hotel, transport, restaurant and other third-party reservations directly.
