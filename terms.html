Terms of Service

Service scope

LORUNEI provides travel research, comparisons, information, recommendations and booking guidance. LORUNEI does not book, purchase, resell, or contract with hotels, transportation providers, restaurants or other travel providers on the customer’s behalf. Customers make, contract and pay for third-party services directly with each provider.

Quick Help

Quick Help is a USD 49 asynchronous written-answer service for one focused Japan travel topic, with up to 3 closely related questions within that topic. No appointment or video call is required. The delivery is intended to provide a clear recommendation, practical reasoning, relevant cautions and a useful next step. Separate unrelated topics or multiple separate travel decisions are outside Quick Help scope. Answers are usually delivered within 2 business days after the information needed to begin has been received.

Japan Answer

Japan Answer is a USD 99 decision-brief service for one important Japan travel decision that requires alternatives, priorities and trade-offs to be considered together. It is not defined as two Quick Help answers combined. The deliverable may include a structured comparison, recommendation, reasoning, trade-offs and practical next steps, depending on the request.

Japan Research

Japan Research is for a question that requires deeper comparison, trade-off analysis or a focused research brief. The exact research question and practical scope are confirmed before work begins.

Private Japan Planning

Private Japan Planning is a custom service for travelers who want their wider journey considered together, including destinations, stays, dining, experiences and logistics. Scope, delivery timing, price and payment terms are confirmed in a written quote before payment.

Quick Help to Japan Answer upgrade

If LORUNEI determines before deeper individualized work begins that a Quick Help request is clearly better suited to Japan Answer, LORUNEI will inform the customer before proceeding. The customer may keep the request within Quick Help scope or, with the customer’s approval, apply the full USD 49 Quick Help payment toward the USD 99 Japan Answer price. The remaining amount is USD 50. No upgrade or additional charge is made without the customer’s approval.

When individualized research begins

Individualized research begins when LORUNEI sends the customer a work-start confirmation email. Refund eligibility tied to the start of research is determined using that written confirmation.

Refunds

A full refund may be requested before individualized research begins. Once individualized research has begun, customer-requested refunds are generally unavailable because the service is customized. If LORUNEI cannot provide an agreed service, the unprovided portion will be refunded or an appropriate alternative will be offered, subject to applicable law.

Revisions and follow-up

If a service description or written quote includes a revision or follow-up period, that stated scope applies. Requests outside the agreed scope, or material changes to dates, destinations, trip length or the original request, may require a new request, quote or additional fee.

Third-party services

Hotels, restaurants, transport operators and other third parties set their own prices, availability, rules and terms. Recommendations and availability information can change, and customers are responsible for reviewing the provider’s current terms before booking or paying.

No emergency service

LORUNEI is not an emergency, medical, legal, immigration, police or disaster-response service. Customers should contact the appropriate official service in emergencies.

クイックヘルプ

クイックヘルプは49米ドルの非同期・文章回答型サービスで、日本旅行に関する1つのテーマについて、関連する質問を3つまで対象とします。予約やビデオ通話は不要です。結論、実用的な理由、必要な注意点、次の一歩を分かりやすくまとめることを目的とします。互いに関係のない別テーマや、複数の独立した旅行判断はクイックヘルプの範囲外です。開始に必要な情報を受領後、通常2営業日以内を目安に回答します。

ジャパン・アンサー

ジャパン・アンサーは99米ドルのDecision Brief型サービスで、候補・条件・メリットとデメリットを一つの問題として比較する必要がある重要な旅行判断を対象とします。Quick Helpを2件組み合わせたサービスではありません。ご依頼に応じて、構造化した比較、おすすめ、理由、トレードオフ、実用的な次の一歩をまとめます。

ジャパン・リサーチ

Japan Researchは、より深い比較、選択肢のメリット・デメリット整理、特定テーマのリサーチが必要な依頼向けのサービスです。具体的な調査テーマと実務上の範囲は、作業開始前に確認します。

プライベート・ジャパン・プランニング

Private Japan Planningは、目的地、宿泊、食事、体験、移動など、旅行全体を一体として検討したい方向けの個別サービスです。具体的な範囲、納期、料金、支払条件は、支払い前の書面による見積で確認します。
