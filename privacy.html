We may collect your name, email address, travel dates, destinations, interests, budget, dietary preferences, accessibility needs and other information you choose to provide for travel research and customer support.

Do not send sensitive credentials

Please do not send passport numbers, payment card numbers, passwords, government ID numbers or other sensitive credentials through our inquiry or intake forms.

If you provide information about another traveler, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis to share that information with us.

How we use information

We use information to answer inquiries, confirm scope, provide purchased services, communicate about delivery and refunds, maintain business records and improve our service.

Service providers

We use or may use service providers such as Formspree for form submissions, Stripe for payment processing, and email/hosting providers to operate the service. Their own privacy terms apply to information they process.

International processing

Some service providers may process information outside your country of residence.

Retention

We keep customer information only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type.

Your requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Contact: infolorunei@gmail.com.
